# Trademarks

The software and documentation in Kokeb Labs Open Boards repositories are MIT licensed. The licence covers the
code and the text, not the **Kokeb Labs** name or the logos. Those identify our projects, and we keep them distinct
so people can tell what came from us. The licence of the hardware design files is recorded in each board's
`board.yml` (see [ADR-0001](docs/adr/0001-hardware-licence.md)) and is separate from the name and logo.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying what your project uses or is based on: "based on board-bmp581-breakout from Kokeb Labs Open Boards".
- Keeping the name in a fork's history, licence and notices.
- Showing the unmodified logo next to a factual reference, such as a list of designs a project uses.

## Ask first

- Using the name or logo in the name of your product, company, domain, repository or package.
- Selling boards, kits or services under the name or logo, or in a way that suggests we made, tested or endorse
  them. A board you build from the open design and sell must not carry our name or imply our verification.
- Changing the logo, or combining it with your own mark.

## Forks and derivatives

A design or fork distributed as its own project must use its own name and logo. The open files stay open under their
licence; only the name and logo change. Claims that a board has been verified, tested or released apply to our
tagged releases and their evidence only, never to a derivative.

Requests: open a discussion at <https://github.com/orgs/kokeb-labs-open-boards/discussions> or contact an
organization owner.
